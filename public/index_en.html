🔒 Privacy and Data Protection Policy (LGPD)

The Brasil Powerlifting Meet Management System (published by Hiroshi Tecnologia) operates by default 100% Offline and Locally.

• All competition and lifter data is stored exclusively on the organizer's computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\BrasilPowerlifting).
• Fields such as ID/CPF, date of birth, WhatsApp, and social media are optional.
• No information is sent to the internet unless the organizer voluntarily configures a synchronization key in the Cloud Center.
• Upon uninstalling the application, all local databases are cleanly and completely removed by Windows.
