🔒 Privacy and Data Protection Policy (LGPD)

The Brasil Powerlifting Meet Management System (published by Hiroshi Tecnologia) respects the privacy of organizers, referees, and lifters in compliance with the General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13.709/2018 - LGPD) and Microsoft Store Policies.

1. 100% Local Storage by Default: All data registered in the system (including name, category, bodyweight, and optional fields such as ID/CPF, date of birth, WhatsApp, social media, and adaptive/para-athlete status) is stored exclusively in the local SQLite database on the organizer's computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\BrasilPowerlifting).

2. Minimal and Optional Collection: No sensitive data is required for running a meet. Fields such as ID/CPF, date of birth, and phone number are strictly optional and used only by the local organizer to identify lifters across competitions.

3. Optional Cloud Synchronization: The application does not send data to external servers without explicit action by the organizer. Remote synchronization only occurs if the organizer voluntarily configures their own URL and Access Key/Token on this screen.

4. Complete Deletion upon Uninstallation: The organizer can delete lifters or meets at any time within the application. Upon uninstalling the software via Windows / Microsoft Store, all local databases and backups are removed from the device.
